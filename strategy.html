Start fib-boundary campaign

L2·L3·L4·L6·L8·L12·L16
The mother candle names where to look — it is no longer the fib anchor itself. The ladder is measured off the swing around it: the leg extreme behind the mother, and the extreme in front frozen by the first involvement (2 consecutive candles the other way). The mother chart sets how wide the swing is. Entries are always 1-minute touches on L2·L3·L4·L6·L8·L12·L16, one lot each, until the ladder cap is spent. The whole basket sells at one target, 0.25 of the way back toward the anchor, and the campaign then ends. No stop loss — only the target or expiry closes it.

PAPER CAMPAIGNS ON LIVE PRICES · NO REAL ORDER IS EVER SENT

Recovery

Two red candles where the SECOND closes below the FIRST's low arm a buy at the second red's high — price must rise back through it, so a recovery is bought and never a falling knife. The stop is the entry candle's own low; a wick through it survives, a close below it does not. After any trade closes the next may only arm by breaking lower, never off a bounce. The target is not a price: the open trade must net back every rupee already lost, costs included, plus a margin. Positions square off at the day's last bar — only the ledger carries overnight.
IDLE
Lots 1 then 2 · stop on the entry candle low · ATM−2 CE, 4+ days to expiry, re-picked at every fill. 15m ladder measured +₹82,896 over Jan–Jul 2026 on mechanical mothers; 5m fib-zone +₹6,732 intraday.
